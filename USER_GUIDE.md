# TempoSight — Quick Start User Guide (One-Page Quick Reference)

**AI-Powered Piano Sight-Reading & Repertoire Diagnostic Companion**  
*For Students, Teachers, and Pianists*

---

## 🎹 Quick 3-Step Practice Workflow

```
[ 1. Select Score ] ──▶ [ 2. Record Performance ] ──▶ [ 3. Get Instant AI Diagnostic ]
   Custom sheet / Samples     Live Mic or Audio Upload      Measure breakdown & PDF report
```

---

### Step 1: Stage Your Sheet Music
1. **Instant Samples (Recommended for first-time use):**  
   Click the **"Sample Pieces"** button to load pre-tested repertoire (*Für Elise*, *Minuet in G Major*, or *Until I Found You*). Both the notation and reference recordings are preloaded for instant evaluation.
2. **Custom Sheet Music:**  
   Click **"Upload Sheet Music"** or drag & drop any image file (`.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.webp`).
3. **Optical Inspection:**  
   Use the **Zoom Slider** (`+` / `-`) and pan controls on the sheet viewer to inspect clefs, key signatures, and measure numbers clearly.

---

### Step 2: Record or Upload Your Playing
1. **Live Microphone Recording:**  
   Click **"Record Audio"** and grant browser microphone permission. Position your device near your piano keyboard. Watch the dynamic waveform visualizer to ensure clear sound capture, then click **"Stop"** when finished.
2. **Audio File Upload:**  
   Alternatively, click **"Upload Audio"** to import an existing recording (`.wav`, `.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.ogg`).
3. **Set Evaluation Tier:**  
   Select your current skill level: **Beginner**, **Intermediate**, or **Advanced** to calibrate the scoring strictness.

---

### Step 3: Run AI Diagnostic & Review Report
Click **"Analyze Practice Session"**. Within seconds, TempoSight evaluates your audio against the score and presents your interactive diagnostic dashboard:

* **Overall Performance Score:** A composite 0–100 score with a letter grade (e.g., `A`, `B+`, `94/100`).
* **4 Core Competency Gauges:**
  * 🎯 **Pitch Accuracy:** Detects wrong notes, missed accidentals, and key signature errors.
  * ⏱️ **Rhythm & Tempo:** Highlights rushed subdivisions, dragging beats, and uneven meter.
  * 🎼 **Key Signature Adherence:** Verifies sharp/flat consistency across registers.
  * 🎹 **Dynamic Expression:** Assesses phrasing, articulation, and balance.
* **Measure-by-Measure Discrepancy Log:** Lists exact bar numbers with specific errors (e.g., *"Measure 4: Right-hand F# played as F natural"*).
* **Targeted Practice Strategies:** Step-by-step isolation drills, hands-separate practice suggestions, and optimal metronome practice tempos.

## 🛠️ Key Productivity Features

| Feature | Where to find it | What it does |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **History Drawer** | Top Header → **"HISTORY"** | Opens your session history. Reload any past evaluation or track your long-term trend chart. |
| **Export PDF Report** | Diagnostic Page → **"Export PDF"** | Generates a clean, printable 1-page practice report to give to your teacher or parents. |
| **TempoSight Coach** | Bottom-Right Floating Button | Interactive AI piano tutor. Ask questions like *"How do I fix the timing in bar 12?"* |
| **Audio Playback** | Diagnostic Page Audio Player | Replay your recorded attempt while viewing the score to hear where mistakes occurred. |
| **Ambient Soundscape** | Top-Right Audio Icon | Toggle soft ambient studio audio for focused, calm practice sessions. |

---

## 💡 Top Tips for Best Results
* **Microphone Placement:** Place your phone/laptop 1–2 meters away from the piano soundboard or speakers to prevent audio distortion.
* **Clear Sheet Photos:** If uploading sheet music photos, ensure good lighting and avoid shadows or heavy page curvature.
* **Focus on Problem Measures:** Use the measure discrepancy log to isolate 2–4 bars with a metronome before re-recording your next attempt.
